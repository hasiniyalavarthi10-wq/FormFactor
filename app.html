Analyze your lift

Not medical advice. Results are an angular risk estimate based on published biomechanics research, not a force or medical measurement. Best accuracy: side view, whole body in frame, good lighting. The camera video is processed inside your browser and never leaves this device.

Set up your camera

  • Stand sideways to the camera, about 2.5–3 meters (8–10 feet) away.
  • Put the camera at about hip height (a laptop on a chair or box works).
  • Your whole body, head to feet, must stay in the picture the whole set.
  • Use bright, even lighting. Avoid a bright window behind you.
