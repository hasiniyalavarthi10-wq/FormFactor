FormFactor

A free website that checks your squat and deadlift form with your webcam. Made for high school athletes who don't have a coach watching them lift, can't afford one, or want to get stronger and feel more confident while still being safe.

Why we made this

We made this website in order to help young athletes hoping to improve their health and body have an easy access way to ensure they do not hurt themselves and get the best results in the gym.

The problem

A lot of high schoolers lift on their own with nobody checking their form. If your chest drops forward too fast when you stand up, your lower back ends up doing the work instead of your legs. A coach would notice, but most teens don't have one.

How to use it

  1. Set up your laptop or phone so it sees you from the side.
  2. Click Start, do your reps, then click Stop.
  3. Watch it back. The lines turn green, yellow, or red, and you get tips on what to fix.
