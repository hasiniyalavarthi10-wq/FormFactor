The problem

Most teen athletes lift with no one checking their form.

Bad technique, like letting your chest drop forward fast as you stand up, moves the work from your legs to your lower back. A coach or physical therapist would catch it. Most high schoolers training on their own don't have one.

How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

1

Set up

Put your laptop or phone at hip height and stand sideways, about 3 meters away.

2

Lift

After a 5-second countdown, do your reps. FormFactor tracks your joints live. Click Stop when you're done.

3

See your results

Watch your set back with green, yellow, or red lines, plus simple tips on what to fix.

Your video stays on your device

Everything runs inside your browser. Your camera video is never uploaded or saved. No account, no sign-up, free. (The browser downloads the pose-detection model from Google once, but nothing is sent back.)

Not medical advice. FormFactor gives an estimate based on your body angles and published biomechanics research. It does not measure force or the curve of your spine. If you have pain, see a doctor or physical therapist.
