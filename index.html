FormFactor Analyze Methodology

Check your squat and deadlift form, free

For teen athletes training without a coach or physical therapist.

The problem

Many high school athletes lift weights with no one watching their form. Poor technique, like letting the chest drop forward fast while the legs straighten, puts extra load on the lower back. A coach would catch it. Most teens don't have one.

What FormFactor does

  1. You upload a side-view video of one squat or deadlift set.
  2. FormFactor finds your shoulder, hip, knee, and ankle in every frame.
  3. It measures your trunk lean, knee angle, and hip angle over time.
  4. It gives a green / yellow / red result with plain-English notes, and shows you the frame that needs the most attention.
Try it now

Your video stays on your device

All analysis runs inside your browser. Your video is never uploaded to any server. (The browser downloads the pose-detection model from Google's servers once, but nothing is sent back.)

Not medical advice. FormFactor gives an angular risk estimate based on published biomechanics research. It does not measure force or spinal flexion directly. If you have pain, see a doctor or physical therapist.
